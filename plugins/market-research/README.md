# Market Research

Market research for technology purchases: survey current products, compare independent evidence, and recommend only when asked.

## Skills

- **Technology Market Research**: Conduct rigorous, decision-oriented market research for technology purchases. Use whenever a user wants help choosing, comparing, shortlisting, or researching tech products such as headphones, laptops, monitors, cameras, peripherals, smart-home devices, software services, or components—especially when they want current models, broad brand coverage, trusted reviews, prices, durability or repairability analysis, and a comparison table before a recommendation.
